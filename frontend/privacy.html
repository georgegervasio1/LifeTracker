Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

The short version: LifeTracker shows you your own finances, health and goals in one place. Your data is used only to run the app for you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and you can disconnect services or have your data deleted at any time.

Who we are

LifeTracker ("LifeTracker", "we", "us") is a personal dashboard application operated by George Gervasio. This policy explains what information LifeTracker collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the LifeTracker website at georgegervasio1.github.io/LifeTracker and its supporting services.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a unique account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Financial information you connect. If you link a bank, card or brokerage account through Plaid, we receive transaction details (date, amount, merchant, category) and investment holdings (security, quantity, value, cost basis). We do not receive or store your bank login credentials; Plaid handles the sign-in. We store a Plaid access token so we can refresh your data.
  • Files you upload. If you import a bank, card or Venmo statement, we store the transactions in it.
  • Health information you connect. If you connect WHOOP, we receive, with your permission, your recovery score, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, day strain, sleep duration and performance, workouts (activity type, duration, strain, heart rate and calories) and basic profile information. We store WHOOP access and refresh tokens so we can keep your data up to date.
  • Information you enter. Workouts, exercises, notes, goals and similar entries you add yourself.
  • AI assistant messages. Questions you ask an in-app assistant, and the parts of your data needed to answer them (see "Service providers").
  • Device preferences. Your light or dark mode choice is saved in your own browser. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use your information

  • To show you your own dashboards, charts, calendars and summaries.
  • To keep connected data current, for example syncing new transactions or WHOOP recovery scores.
  • To answer questions you ask the AI assistant about your own data.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. We do not use your data to train AI models.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run LifeTracker. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Cloud and Firebase for sign-in, application hosting and the database that stores your data.
  • GitHub Pages to host the website's code. Your personal data is not stored there.
  • Plaid to connect financial accounts you choose to link.
  • WHOOP to retrieve health data you choose to connect.
  • Anthropic to power the AI assistant. When you ask a question, your message and the relevant data needed to answer it are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the service.

How we protect your information

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and data is encrypted at rest in Google Cloud.
  • The database cannot be read directly from a browser. Only LifeTracker's server can access it, and only after verifying your sign-in on every request.
  • Each user's data is stored separately and is only returned to that user.
  • Service credentials and API keys are kept in Google Secret Manager, not in the website's code.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of any breach as required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data while your account is active so the app can show your history.
  • You can disconnect WHOOP at any time from Health → Connect, which stops syncing and revokes LifeTracker's access. To disconnect a linked financial account, contact us. Data already synced remains in your account until you ask us to delete it.
  • You can delete workouts you entered directly in the app.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below. We will delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep information to meet a legal obligation.

Your choices and rights

You can access your data in the app at any time. You may request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your data by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor requests as required by those laws.

Children

LifeTracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app before the changes take effect.
